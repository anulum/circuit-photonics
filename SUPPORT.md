# Support

For questions about project scope, licensing or contributions, contact Miroslav Šotek, ANULUM, at protoscience@anulum.li.

The repository foundation is under construction. There is no released package, photonic design or simulation service, installation support commitment or qualified photonic cell library. Do not rely on planned records for a waveguide-loss, phase-tuning, thermo-optic crosstalk, laser-safety or tapeout decision.

For a technical report, provide the exact source revision, runtime environment, expected behaviour and minimal legally shareable input; never attach a customer design, a confidential photonic foundry kit or an optical measurement that is not public. Keep original outcomes and state which references or environments were unavailable. See [contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
